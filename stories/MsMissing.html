Ms. Missing

A weight is missing on my shoulder

Not a boulder

a girl,

Sometimes with lovely curls.

In bed,

I often find me in my head

Forgetting she’s not there,

wrapped around me, chests so bare

I miss,

telling me to take it off

me bearing all my shame I almost

scoff,

But she had done the same.

And light who walked in through the window

flitters,

Not like our fruit which grows too old and

Bitters.

I miss,

Serene this scene which seems a dream

her eyes were closed and mine observing.

What I saw: a pretti–er thing

ever was the pleasure

Of pleasure–uring.

I miss,

my Ms.

Bring me back to that day

So very long and far away

See me smile like not before

For only then

did I say

of life:

“I want this more”
